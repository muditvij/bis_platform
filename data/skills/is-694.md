# IS 694 - PVC Insulated Cables for Working Voltages up to 1100 V (Electric Wires)

IS 694 governs PVC insulated unsheathed and sheathed electric wires and cables with copper or aluminium conductors for working voltages up to 1100 Volts. The standard specifies conductor resistance, insulation thickness, heat resistance, flame retardance (FR / FRLS properties), and spark testing. It is the mandatory benchmark for all domestic residential and commercial building wiring.

## सारांश (Summary Hindi)
IS 694 1100 वोल्ट तक के घरेलू और व्यावसायिक वायरिंग के लिए पीवीसी इंसुलेटेड तारों और केबलों (Electric Wire / Cable) का अनिवार्य मानक है। यह तांबे के तार की चालकता, इंसुलेशन मोटाई, अग्निरोधक (FR / FRLS) गुणों और बिजली शॉर्ट-सर्किट सुरक्षा को नियंत्रित करता है।

## Next Steps
1. Inspect wire coils to ensure the continuous embossing of 'IS 694', conductor size, and ISI mark
2. Verify that copper conductor resistance conforms to the maximum limits in IS 8130
3. Prefer Flame Retardant Low Smoke (FRLS) certified wires for enhanced building fire safety
